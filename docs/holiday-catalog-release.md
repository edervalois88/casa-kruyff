# Holiday catalog release 11

Sources: user-provided nutcrackers1.zip and nutcrackers2.zip. Eight selected catalog images were edited with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP. Originals, measurements sheet, label photographs and generation source files remain outside the published gallery.

Edit brief: preserve the exact product silhouette, proportions, color and finish; replace surroundings with a warm pale beige studio backdrop and matte surface; use soft diffused light, realistic contact shadows, sharp detail, portrait framing and consistent margins. One product per photo except the three white silhouettes supplied as a grouped reference. The obscured crowned gold model uses an upper-body detail rather than invented hidden feet/base.

Only the wooden horse has an explicitly supported material. Nutcracker substrate and product-specific measurements remain unconfirmed. The measurement sheet has no reliable model-to-measurement mapping or visible units. The trio is described as a reference selection, with individual pieces and sizes to confirm.

Sandra Roldán necklace data was supplied directly by the user: sewing thread, crystals and filigree; knot-based weaving, handcrafted embroidery and filigree; made by older women artisans from Antioquia, Colombia. Accessory IDs 14 and 15 move to the end without changing photos or dimensions.

Validation: catalog/CMS/admin tests, TypeScript, image existence, release idempotence, preservation of later edits and ordering of necklaces.
