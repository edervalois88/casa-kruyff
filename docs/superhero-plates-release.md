# Super Hero Collection and Caroline Rovithi plates

Release 20 reuses the previously unpublished piece-18 for Elder Superman and adds three individually framed portraits and four hand-painted ceramic plates. Existing art remains in Art & Prints; plates are in Ceramics & Pottery. Collection names are included in portrait titles.

Client-confirmed facts: all four portraits originate in San Miguel de Allende, Mexico. Only Elder Superman is attributed to Carlos Gunter, with dimensions 101.6 × 152.4 cm. Wonder Woman, Batman and Hulk have no confirmed artist; each is 75 × 53.5 cm print only and 82 × 61 cm framed. No dimensions were supplied for the plates.

Plate copy follows the supplied updated description sheet (pieces 41–44). The Casa Kruyff friendship example is listed as Made to Order Plate, with copy explaining that each commission is individual. Each plate retains original reference photographs alongside its studio presentation.

## Image editing

Built-in image_gen was used for Hulk and four plate studio photographs. Prompt: preserve the exact artwork, inscriptions, signatures, frame/design and colors; replace photographic surroundings with a warm beige studio wall/surface, diffuse light, subtle shadow, centered complete product with margins; correct perspective and photographic haze without redesigning the artwork. Outputs were visually compared against the supplied references and encoded as WebP in public/images/catalog.

Three portrait edits did not complete; a separate Batman edit was explicitly rejected by the image service. Wonder Woman, Batman and Elder Superman therefore use the supplied originals, encoded as WebP without a new generated interpretation. Their studio restyling is not complete.
